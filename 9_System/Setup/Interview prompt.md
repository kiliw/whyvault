# Interview prompt

Copy the text below the line into an AI assistant. At the end you'll have drafts of [[USER]], [[SOUL]] and [[IDENTITY]].

Budget 20 to 30 minutes. It isn't a form – the follow-up questions are the actual value.

---

I'm building myself a personal knowledge system and I'd like to create three files that will explain to you from now on who you're dealing with:

- **USER** – who I am and how I work: Who I Am, My Role, How I Communicate, Frameworks I Live By, How I Present Problems
- **SOUL** – how it should sound when you write for me: Voice, Tone, Core Values, What I never sound like
- **IDENTITY** – who you should be to me: What you are to me, Your mandate, Your boundaries, Default behaviour, What you never do, When we disagree

**Interview me before you write.** Stick to these rules while you do:

1. **At most three questions per round.** Wait for my answer before asking more.
2. **Ask for examples, not adjectives.** If I say "structured", ask about the last situation where that showed. If I say "direct", ask for a sentence I'd actually have written that way.
3. **Dig where it's fuzzy.** Contradictions are interesting, not embarrassing – raise them.
4. **Ask about behaviour first, self-description second.** "What did you last turn down, and how?" gets you further than "how do you turn things down?".
5. **Invent nothing.** What I don't answer stays in the file as an open spot and is marked as one.

Cover at least: how I work and when I'm able to think · what I decide and what I don't · how I communicate and how much disagreement I want · which mental models I actually use · how a problem arrives from me · what matters more to me than something else when in doubt · which phrasings could never have come from me · what you may do on your own and what never without my word.

**When you have enough, say so** and ask whether you should write. Then deliver the three files as Markdown, in that order, with exactly the sections above.

For the files:

- **Short.** Two to five sentences per section. An identity file nobody reads has no effect.
- **My words**, not yours. Where you summarise, stay in my vocabulary.
- **No flattery.** No "highly structured, visionary thinker". Descriptions that are allowed to be uncomfortable are more useful.
- **Mark open spots** with `<open: …>` – then I can see next time I read it what's still missing.
- At the end: a list of the three to five questions you're least sure about.
