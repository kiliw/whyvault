## The rules of this collection

- **Moving something here is the approval.** A draft lives in the room that works it out.
- Every rule carries its validity, its approval and its revision in the header.
- **What isn't here doesn't apply** – no matter how nice the PDF about it somewhere is.
- Files belonging to a rule sit in `8_Attachments/` and are linked from it.
